- Frontmatter `keyTakeaway`: 1-2 sentence citable answer (GEO).
- Direct answer in the first 2-3 sentences (featured snippet).
- ≥3 internal links (pillar + money pages), ≥1 list, table where natural.
- No invented statistics or fake customers. First-hand product perspective at least once.
- Title ≤60 chars with target keyword; meta description 140-160 chars.
