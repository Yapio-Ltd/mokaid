# Competitor Analysis — AI Employee / AI Workforce Market (August 2026)

Research basis: public positioning, product pages, and third-party reviews as of August 2026.
Refresh quarterly — this market moves fast.

## Market map

The "AI employees" market splits into three clusters:

1. **Sales-specialist digital workers** — Artisan (Ava, AI BDR), 11x (Alice AI SDR, Julian AI
   phone agent). Enterprise-priced, sales-led, single-function.
2. **General no-code assistant platforms** — Lindy (workflow-built "Lindies"), Sintra
   (marketplace of pre-configured helpers), Sistava (role-based employees with governance).
3. **Enterprise agent platforms** — Salesforce Agentforce ("digital labor"), Microsoft
   Agent 365 (agents with Entra IDs). Massive reach, but sold as suite add-ons.

**Nobody in any cluster offers a visual/spatial representation of the AI workforce.** That is
mokaid's uncontested position: the 3D office as the management interface.

## Competitor profiles

### Lindy (lindy.ai)
- **Positioning:** "first platform letting you build a team of AI employees"; no-code builder.
- **SEO strengths:** strong blog (comparisons, listicles, how-tos), high domain authority,
  freemium funnel, ranks broadly on assistant/automation queries.
- **Weaknesses to exploit:** "employee" framing is thin (they are workflows); no persistent
  role/office metaphor; personal-ops focus more than company workforce.
- **Our counter:** /compare/mokaid-vs-lindy + role pages that emphasize outcome ownership,
  governance, and visibility.

### Artisan (artisan.co)
- **Positioning:** "AI employees" for outbound sales; Ava the AI BDR; Y Combinator, $25M+ raised.
- **SEO strengths:** category-term ads + PR volume; "hire AI employees" association.
- **Weaknesses to exploit:** single function (outbound), no public pricing, mixed reviews
  (G2 ~3.5-3.8, "AI slop" complaints), enterprise-only motion.
- **Our counter:** /compare/mokaid-vs-artisan; self-serve + multi-role + approval-gate quality
  control as the antidote to AI slop.

### 11x (11x.ai)
- **Positioning:** "digital workers" for enterprise revenue teams (AI SDR + AI phone agent).
- **SEO strengths:** brand PR, "digital worker" term association, enterprise case studies.
- **Weaknesses to exploit:** no self-serve, no transparency (pricing/capabilities), sales-only.
- **Our counter:** /compare/mokaid-vs-11x; transparency and breadth story.

### Sintra (sintra.ai)
- **Positioning:** friendly pre-built AI "helpers" for solopreneurs/SMB growth.
- **SEO strengths:** volume content, cheap entry point, personality-driven brand.
- **Weaknesses to exploit:** helpers assist rather than own outcomes; light governance;
  prompting-centric.
- **Our counter:** /compare/mokaid-vs-sintra; "past prompting → delegating outcomes" narrative.
